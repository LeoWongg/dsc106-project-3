How did the West's wildfires spread, hour by hour?

Every square is one satellite pixel, about 2 by 3 km, where GOES-18 saw fire between July 13 and August 31, 2026. Detections seen only once in two hours are dropped as false alarms. Five-minute scans are averaged into hours of local solar time. Power for cloud-flagged pixels is estimated from their neighbours.
